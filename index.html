Kitchen Fusion Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026 · applies to the Kitchen Fusion app on iOS and Android

The short version

  • You don't need an account to play. If you play online, the game makes an anonymous player ID and keeps a copy of your progress on our game server. Saving it with Apple or Google is optional, and you can delete your online data from the game.
  • Online, other players only see a name the game makes from its own word lists, a chef picture, scores and points. There's no free chat and no search for players.
  • Before anything online, the game asks for a month and year of birth. It stays on your phone. Players under 13 play online only after a grown-up says yes.
  • Kitchen Fusion is free because it shows ads. Our ad partners (Unity LevelPlay and the networks it works with) collect device data to show and measure those ads.
  • You choose whether ads may be personalized: on iPhone through the tracking prompt, through the in-game privacy prompt when shown, and for everyone through PRIVACY CHOICES in Settings.
  • Apple and Google handle all payments. We never see your card details.

1Who we are

Kitchen Fusion is a cooking puzzle game made by Puffy Interactive, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. We are responsible for the game and for information you send to us. Our service providers and advertising partners also process data under their own policies (see section 7).

3Playing online

The leaderboards, Kitchen Crews and friends are online. To take part, the game signs in to our game server (Unity Gaming Services) with an anonymous player ID and keeps a copy of your progress there, so it can come back on a new phone. If you save your progress with Apple or Google, your Apple or Google account is linked to that player ID; the game receives only a sign-in token, not your name or email address.

  • Names: every player's name is made from the game's own word lists (for example "Sunny Pancake"). Nothing is typed, so no real names appear.
  • No free chat: crews talk with preset stickers only. Friends are added with a friend code made from the same word lists, shared outside the game. There's no player search.
  • Report and block: you can report or block any player. Reports go to a private queue only we can read. A player reported by several others is hidden from crews and friend requests.
  • Age: before anything online, the game asks for a month and year of birth. That answer stays on your phone; the server only keeps whether online play is allowed and whether a grown-up said yes (see section 9).
  • Deleting it: see section 10.

4Ads and your choices

The game shows rewarded videos you choose to watch (for example to double a day's takings or get a second chance) and occasional full-screen ads between shifts. Buying No Ads or the Starter Bundle removes the between-shift ads.

Personalized ads

  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks whether it may track you across apps (App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, your advertising ID is not shared. You can change this in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent choices: Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents an advertising consent form where required. You can review it in Settings › PRIVACY CHOICES › Manage consent choices when available.
  • Everyone, including US residents: Settings › PRIVACY CHOICES › Do not sell or share my data sends an opt-out to LevelPlay and enables Google restricted data processing. Existing opt-outs are preserved. You can remove this extra limit later; doing so does not override a separate consent refusal or your phone’s tracking settings.

Meta campaign measurement

Versions with the Facebook SDK can send app activation and selected gameplay events to Meta to measure our advertising campaigns. No Facebook login is required. We do not forward purchase receipts, friend codes, birth dates or arbitrary error messages to Meta. Automatic purchase instrumentation is disabled.

Meta measurement starts only after the advertising privacy flow and the iOS tracking decision, where applicable. The iOS advertising identifier is collected only with tracking authorization. Meta app events remain disabled in regions requiring a consent form, including the UK, EEA and Switzerland, pending a separate Meta consent option. The global Do not sell or share my data choice stops further Meta gameplay events. Players identified by the age screen as under 13 do not send Meta events.

Ads for basic delivery, frequency capping, counting and fraud prevention still use some device data. The partners' policies explain their processing practices and legal bases. Turning personalization off does not stop all advertising-related data processing.

5In-app purchases

Purchases (No Ads, the Starter Bundle, currency packs, the Cookie Jar, season passes, cosmetics and the Golden Apron Club subscription) use Apple or Google payment processing under their own terms and privacy policies. Unity In-App Purchasing connects the game to those stores and may process service identifiers, device information and purchase-related technical data. We receive confirmation that a purchase happened and a transaction ID, which we keep on your device so a purchase is never delivered twice and can be restored. The game does not receive your payment card details. The optional Golden Apron Club renews monthly until cancelled through your store account; its localized price and renewal terms are shown before purchase. Premium season rewards include random sticker packs; the purchase screens link to the current sticker probabilities.

6Data on your device

Your progress and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your phone (and, if you play online, copied to our game server: section 3), including a backup copy that protects your save if the app closes unexpectedly. Your month and year of birth stay on your phone. If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders locally on your phone (the daily treat, the lunch rush). No notification server is involved and you can turn reminders off in Settings.

8Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data held about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to or restrict its use, receive a copy, and withdraw consent at any time. US residents may also have the right to know what is collected, ask for deletion, and opt out of sale or sharing, without being treated differently for using these rights.

You can delete your online data in the game (section 10). For game and advertising choices, start with the controls above: PRIVACY CHOICES in Settings, your phone's tracking and advertising ID settings, and uninstalling the app. For data held by our ad partners, use their privacy policies linked in section 7. You can also email us and we'll help or pass your request on. If you're in the EU, EEA or UK you can complain to your local data protection authority.

9Children

Kitchen Fusion is a general-audience game. Before anything online, it asks for a month and year of birth (the answer stays on the phone). For a player under 13:

  • our ad partner is told the player is a child, so ads aren't personalized;
  • the game's analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase and Meta app events) are switched off on that phone, so no gameplay events are sent;
  • the leaderboards show the island's own characters, not real players, and crews and friends stay off, unless a grown-up says yes behind a grown-ups' check in PARENTS & SAFETY;
  • with a grown-up's yes, online play works as in section 3: word-list names, preset stickers and no free chat. A grown-up can turn it off again in PARENTS & SAFETY.

A parent or guardian can ask us to review or delete a child's online data at the address below, or delete it in the game (section 10).

10Keeping and deleting data

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Your online data (the player ID, the copy of your progress, your current scores, crew and friends) is kept while you play online. To delete it, open the account card (Settings › SAVED WITH APPLE or SAVED WITH GOOGLE) and choose Delete my online data: the game leaves your crew, removes you from your friends' lists, frees your friend code and deletes your player account and the data kept with it. Your island on the phone stays. Historical leaderboard snapshots are separate: the provider’s per-player purge does not remove archived rankings. Contact us about archived entries so we can request removal through the service provider. Uninstalling the app doesn't delete online data; if you haven't saved with Apple or Google, email us and we'll delete it. Reports are kept in the moderation queue, which holds the most recent 1,000. Ad partners keep data for as long as their own policies describe. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google. Uninstalling may remove local progress; device backups and records held by service providers can remain. We keep support correspondence for as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Eligible permanent purchases can be restored with Restore purchases in the shop; previously spent coins are not restored.
