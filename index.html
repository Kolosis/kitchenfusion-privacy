Kitchen Fusion Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026 · applies to the Kitchen Fusion app on iOS and Android

The short version

  • There's no account to make. Your game progress lives on your phone, not on our servers.
  • Kitchen Fusion is free because it shows ads. Our ad partners (Unity LevelPlay and the networks it works with) collect device data to show and measure those ads.
  • You choose whether ads may be personalised: on iPhone through the tracking prompt, through the in-game privacy prompt when shown, and for everyone with the Personal ads switch in Settings.
  • Apple and Google handle all payments. We never see your card details.

1Who we are

Kitchen Fusion is a cooking puzzle game made by Puffy Interactive, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. We are responsible for the game and for information you send to us. Our service providers and advertising partners also process data under their own policies (see section 6).

3Ads and your choices

The game shows rewarded videos you choose to watch (for example to double a day's takings or get a second chance) and occasional full-screen ads between shifts. Buying No Ads or the Starter Bundle removes the between-shift ads.

Personalised ads

  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks whether it may track you across apps (App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, your advertising ID is not shared. You can change this in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • In-game privacy prompt: when your device region is set to the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland, or the region cannot be read, the game asks for an advertising choice before starting the ad service. You can choose either option. A regional setting is not a location measurement.
  • Everyone: the Personal ads switch in the game's Settings (the menu button on the island) turns personalised ads on or off at any time, and withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it.
  • US residents: turning Personal ads off sends a do-not-sell-or-share signal to LevelPlay for targeted advertising. You can also use your phone's advertising controls and contact us about a privacy request.

Ads for basic delivery, frequency capping, counting and fraud prevention still use some device data. The partners' policies explain their processing practices and legal bases. Turning personalisation off does not stop all advertising-related data processing.

4In-app purchases

Purchases (No Ads, the Starter Bundle and coin packs) use Apple or Google payment processing under their own terms and privacy policies. Unity In-App Purchasing connects the game to those stores and may process service identifiers, device information and purchase-related technical data. We receive confirmation that a purchase happened and a transaction ID, which we keep on your device so a purchase is never delivered twice and can be restored. The game does not receive your payment card details.

5Data on your device

Your progress and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your phone, including a backup copy that protects your save if the app closes unexpectedly. If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders locally on your phone (the daily treat, the lunch rush). No notification server is involved and you can turn reminders off in Settings.

6Who we share data with

  • Unity Technologies (Unity LevelPlay ad mediation, Unity Ads, Unity In-App Purchasing, the Unity engine): Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.
  • Google (Google Analytics for Firebase), which receives the game's usage events: Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.
  • ironSource, part of Unity, which runs LevelPlay: ironSource Mobile Privacy Policy.
  • The launch advertising setup uses Unity Ads and the ironSource network through LevelPlay. If we add another network, we will update this policy and the relevant store disclosures.
  • Apple (privacy policy) and Google (privacy policy) for app distribution and payments. Game reminders are scheduled locally through the operating system.

These partners may process data outside your country, including in the United States, using safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

7Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data held about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to or restrict its use, receive a copy, and withdraw consent at any time. US residents may also have the right to know what is collected, ask for deletion, and opt out of sale or sharing, without being treated differently for using these rights.

For game and advertising choices, start with the controls above: the Personal ads switch, your phone's tracking and advertising ID settings, and uninstalling the app. For data held by our ad partners, use their privacy policies linked in section 6. You can also email us and we'll help or pass your request on. If you're in the EU, EEA or UK you can complain to your local data protection authority.

8Children

Kitchen Fusion is a general-audience game and isn't directed at children under 13. We tell our ad partners the game isn't child-directed, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we'll work with our partners to delete it.

9Keeping and deleting data

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Ad partners keep data for as long as their own policies describe. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google. Uninstalling may remove local progress; device backups and records held by service providers can remain. We keep support correspondence for as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Eligible permanent purchases can be restored with Restore purchases in the shop; previously spent coins are not restored.

10Changes to this policy

If what the game collects changes, for example a new ad partner, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
