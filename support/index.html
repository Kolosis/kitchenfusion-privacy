Common questions

I bought No Ads or the Starter Bundle on another phone. Open the shop on the island (the coin button) and tap Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account.

Can I turn off personalised ads? Yes: Settings (the menu button on the island) › Personal ads.

Where is my progress saved? On your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

How do I stop reminders? Settings › Reminders, or your phone's notification settings.
